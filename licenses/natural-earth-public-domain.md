# Natural Earth geographic data

PastPins uses Natural Earth's 1:10m Admin 1 – States, Provinces dataset, version
5.1.1, from the v5.1.2 release of natural-earth-vector.

Made with Natural Earth. Free vector and raster map data at
https://www.naturalearthdata.com/.

Natural Earth dedicates its raster and vector map data to the public domain.
Personal, educational, and commercial use, modification, and redistribution are
permitted without requesting permission. The authors provide no guarantee of
accuracy or fitness for a particular use.

Terms: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/

Source: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/downloads/10m-cultural-vectors/10m-admin-1-states-provinces/

Pinned release: https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/tree/f1890d9f152c896d250a77557a5751a93d494776

The application derives names and IDs from the source attributes and projects,
simplifies, and rounds its geometry for mobile display. Natural Earth's default
boundaries reflect its de facto viewpoint. This is a cartographic dataset, not a
current legal or governmental register of subdivisions.
