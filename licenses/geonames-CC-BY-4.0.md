PastPins uses the GeoNames cities500, admin1CodesASCII and admin2Codes exports.
The data is filtered to current settlements and adapted into an offline search
catalog. Region associations combine source identifiers with Natural Earth
boundaries. No geographic records are manually overridden.

GeoNames data is provided as is, without a warranty of accuracy, timeliness,
or completeness. Source documentation and attribution:
https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/readme.txt
